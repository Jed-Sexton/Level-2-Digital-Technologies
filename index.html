ABOUT

Planr is a productivity and organisational platform, designed to help students to manage their time, tasks, and studies. Planr combines essential tools such as task management, a study timer, a place for note-taking, and telling the students their needed equipment all into one cohesive and integrated website. By combining these tools, Planr reduces the need for students to switch between multiple apps, helping students to stay organised and productive in one single space.

A key focus for Planr is usability and simplicity. The user interface is minimal to reduce the distractions and making it more accessible for students to switch between the different tools. This helps to ensure that Planr helps with students' productivity rather than complicating it.
